ABOUT JM CAKES

CAKES THAT CELEBRATE LIFE

We bake all our cakes and sweets fresh in store daily, using only the finest local ingredients. Whether you’re popping in for a slice or planning a big celebration, we offer a cozy space for catch-ups, family time, or a well-deserved treat.

At JM Cake, every cake is more than just dessert—it’s a centerpiece, a lasting taste, and sometimes even the surprise itself. Whether it’s a milestone birthday cake, a show-stopping wedding creation, or a thoughtful corporate cake, our cakes are made with love, care, and creativity. We proudly serve as a leading bakery in Hobart, delivering sweetness across Hobart and nearby suburbs. From custom cakes to vegan treats, we cater to all tastes, styles, and dietary needs.
