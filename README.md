# JM Cakes - Artisan Bakery & Luxury Cake Atelier

A luxury, interactive e-commerce and showcase website for **JM Cakes**, faithfully created based on the provided reference design.

## ✨ Features & Architecture

1. **Design System & Visual Fidelity**
   - **Rich Chocolate & Burgundy Palette**: Carefully matched to the reference image (`#3d161c`, `#2a0f13`, `#4e1c24`).
   - **Warm Golden Sand & Champagne Creams**: (`#fbf5ee`, `#f3e5d3`) used for contrast cards and typography.
   - **Floating Elements**: 3D CSS isometric chocolate blocks and floating ripe strawberries with physics-inspired keyframe floating animations.
   - **Melted Chocolate Splashes**: Scalable vector art swirls framing the multi-tier drip cake centerpiece.
   - **Rotated Vertical Tag**: "ANY TYPE OF CAKE YOU NEED" anchored to the left viewport.

2. **Core Sections Matching Reference UI**
   - **Hero Section**: Centered luxury typography, brand narrative, CTAs ("SHOP NOW", "EXPLORE MORE"), and the signature Belgian Truffle Drip Cake display.
   - **Prompt Banner**: *"FOR EVERY OCCASION DESIGNED JUST FOR YOU"* with the *"ORDER NOW FOR CUSTOMIZED CAKE"* button.
   - **Occasions Carousel**: Quick-navigation cards for Birthday Cakes, Wedding Cakes, Holiday, and Anniversary specials (clicking filters the live catalog immediately).
   - **"Cakes That Celebrate Life" (Warm Cream Card)**:
     - Prominent chocolate ribbon bow cake visual
     - Brand story & Tasmanian bakery heritage
     - Award badge & master pâtissier signature
     - Micro-feature thumbnails for fresh fruit, custom fondants, and handmade layers
   - **"Ready to Bring Your Dream Cake to Life?"**:
     - Dark chocolate backdrop with realistic angled Polaroid cards featuring previous custom cake orders.
   - **"Special Dietary Options"**:
     - Central strawberry drip cake pedestal with floating cards for Gluten-Free, Vegan & Eggless, Nut-Free, and Sugar Conscious choices.
     - Fully clickable to filter by specific dietary preference!
   - **Interactive Menu / Cake Catalog**:
     - Real-time search by keyword (flavor, dietary, occasion)
     - Filter tabs (All, Chocolate & Truffle, Birthday, Wedding, Holiday & Festive, Anniversary, Gluten-Free & Vegan, Pastries & Cupcakes)
     - Quick View modal for inspecting allergens, slice servings, and flavor notes.
   - **Interactive 3D / Live Cake Customizer Modal**:
     - Select tiers (1-tier, 2-tier, 3-tier)
     - Select sponge flavors (Belgian Chocolate, Vanilla Bean, Red Velvet, Matcha)
     - Choose luxury drip toppings (Chocolate, 24k Gold Caramel, Raspberry Ruby, Naked)
     - Cake crown toppings (Fresh Berries & Gold Flakes, Chocolate Macarons, Edible Roses, Whipped Rosettes)
     - Live custom chocolate inscription plaque preview on top of the cake in real-time
     - Dietary toggles (GF, Vegan, Nut-free)
     - Instant automated pricing and direct "Add to Bag" integration.
   - **Slide-out Shopping Bag Drawer**:
     - Quantity controls and item removal
     - Free delivery progress bar (unlocks free delivery over $120)
     - Fulfillment preference (Salamanca Atelier Pickup, Chilled Hobart Courier, Regional)
     - Full checkout flow with date selection and order confirmation.
   - **Consultation Booking & Tasting Request**: Complete event planning inquiry form with date picker and guest count.
